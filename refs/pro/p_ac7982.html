La Pavoni Copper & Brass Professional, model PB-16.  A stunning work of art.  In 1975 la Pavoni introduced the larger “Professional” model into their line-up next to the smaller “Europiccola”. The larger 16 cup version with the added pressure gauge was an instant success.  All plated la Pavoni machines come standard with 2 frothing systems, a standard plated steam wand and the Cappuccino Automatic attachment.

  • 38oz. boiler capacity
  • Makes one or two cups at a time
  • Dual frothing
  • Mounted pressure gauge
  • Piston operated
  • Internal thermostat to control pressure
  • Nickel plated, solid brass boilers
  • Internal re-set switch in case of overheating
  • 1 year warranty
  • Volt 110, Watts 1000
  • Weight, 16 lbs. Height 12"
  • Made in Italy
  • Gift Boxed
