# Video observations: "Comeback of an Italian Coffee Legend - La Pavoni Restomod" (NTR, J31RKk_7p68)
Machine: La Pavoni Europiccola, production stamp "2 79" (Feb 1979) on base underside -> Ceccarelli v2.2 (1978-83:
double rocker switch, heating element flange fixed with 3 bolts). Pre-millennium group (2-bolt group, no brass sleeve).
Original base colour brown/bronze (painted steel), restored RAL 6027 light green powder coat (180 C x 15 min, primer + top).

## Disassembly order seen (01:30-07:10)
- Cap (black bakelite 6-lobe star knob) unscrewed; brass threaded insert + black rubber cap gasket.
- Steam valve knob (black 6-lobe star, smaller) -> pin driven out (drift) -> stem, packing nut (hex), 2 thin washers + 1 thick rubber packing.
- Steam wand (chromed brass tube, double bend) with union hex nut; steam tip (ball-ended, single hole); steam valve body (hex) in boiler side boss.
- Lever: pins with E-clips (2 pins, 4 E-clips), lever fork (chromed steel), 2 links? (fork plate), black ribbed plastic handle on threaded end.
- Group: removed by 2 hex bolts through oval flange into boiler boss; L-shaped (galvanized/brass) water tube inside boiler port pulled out (02:26);
  green verdigris at flange - copper gasket at flange. Internal circlip at top of cylinder (circlip pliers 02:37), shaft seal + washer.
- Piston: brass, 2 grooves, 2 black lip seals + spacer; seals hard/brittle (03:19). Piston rod chromed steel, threaded end (to piston), cross-hole (lever link pin).
- Portafilter: chromed brass body, "wing" spout (wing-nut shaped, threaded), double + single baskets, basket spring clip, plastic handle on threaded stud.
- Group gasket: black O-ring (~58 mm OD per kit lists) + stainless shower screen cup.
- Sight glass: glass tube in slotted chrome sleeve, 2 hex glass retainers, 2 elbow blocks w/ threaded spigots into boiler, inner hex lock nuts,
  fibre/brass washers, rubber glass seals, slotted top plug screw (10 mm). Lime-blocked; lower nuts reached from inside boiler (06:24-06:57).
- Electrics: 2-gang rocker switch (red I lamp / white II), cable gland (old black plastic strain relief), power cord w/ Schuko plug,
  glass-fibre-sleeved wires w/ insulated spade/ring terminals, red round La Pavoni badge (2 soldered pins, removed with hot air 05:04).
- Heating element: from below, 3 socket screws; copper double coil on brass flange; element gasket.
- Brass ring nut (with 3 ears) holds boiler to base, very tight (self-made tool 05:39; reassembled with 3D-printed tool 23:19).
- Base gaskets: upper + lower flat fibre gaskets (grey/black); one broken (06:04).
- Bottom: black rubber base cover / tray (oval, with lip), stainless perforated drip grid (approx 70 holes, round).

## Assembly details (22:01-30:25)
- Food-grade Loctite (green) on threads; PTFE (white) flat washers on sight-glass spigots; inner nuts tightened via long box tool.
- Base: lower gasket -> base sheet -> ring nut (3 ears) onto boiler bottom thread; upper gasket on top.
- Element gasket -> element flange -> 3 socket-head cap screws into ring-nut ears (two stacked brass flanges visible 23:48).
- Element terminals: ceramic insulated spade terminals (3 + earth), central copper pad.
- New metal cable gland (nickel-plated brass), wrench tightened; earth ring terminal to element flange screw.
- Switch snapped into rectangular cutout in base side.
- Badge pins through 2 holes, bent inside.
- Rubber base cover pressed over base lip (holes for feet/vent).
- Sight glass: PTFE seals, brass washers, slotted sleeve, glass, hex retainers, slotted top plug, 10 mm spanner.
- Group: internal circlip + shaft seal into group top; piston w/ 2 new black seals + brass spacer; silicone grease (manufacturer);
  piston pushed up into cylinder from below; shower screen + group O-ring pressed in (seated later by portafilter).
- Group flange: copper gasket + brass L-tube into port; 2 stainless (A2-70 marking) hex bolts, 12-pt ring spanner.
- Group top stud: hex lock nut + acorn nut (Loctite).
- Lever fork onto group clevis, 2 pins tapped with mallet, E-clips.
- Lever handle: wood (birdseye maple) threaded onto lever end with Loctite.
- Steam wand tip, union nut 16-17 mm spanner; steam valve stem w/ brass washer + rubber packing, packing nut; wooden knob pinned to stem.
- Boiler cap: brass insert (ext. thread + flange) + black rubber cap seal + custom stainless screw + wooden knob.
- Portafilter: stainless threaded rod (blue thread-lock patch) + wooden handle; spout.
- Testing: switch I red lamp (lower power), II white; 14 g dose; double spout.
